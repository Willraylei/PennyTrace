常见问题FAQ

你可能想知道You might be wondering

需要注册账号吗？Do I need an account?

不需要。打开就能用，没有登录，也没有广告。

No. Open the app and start. There's no sign-in and no ads.

我的收据照片会被上传吗？Are my receipt photos uploaded?

不会。照片不会发送给开发者或 AI 服务。开启 AI 后，只有识别出的文字会经云端交给 AI 整理。

No. Photos are never sent to the developer or an AI service. With AI turned on, only the recognized text is sent for processing.

不开 AI 能用吗？Can I use it without AI?

可以。AI 默认关闭，你可以一直手动记账、导入账单，统计、搜索、预算等功能都照常使用。拍照识别需要开启 AI。

Yes. AI is off by default. Manual entry, statement import, stats, search and budgets all work without it. Receipt scanning needs AI turned on.

14 天试用到期会自动扣费吗？Will the 14-day trial charge me?

不会。试用不需要订阅，到期后自动回到免费版，已有的账单都还在。

No. The trial doesn't need a subscription. When it ends you're back on Free, and all your expenses are still there.

能导入微信和支付宝的账单吗？Can I import WeChat Pay or Alipay statements?

可以。从微信或支付宝导出账单文件后，用帐旅打开即可。其他 CSV 和 Excel 表格也支持，导入前可以预览和调整。

Yes. Export the statement from WeChat or Alipay and open it in PennyTrace. Other CSV and Excel files work too, with a preview before import.

支持哪些设备？Which devices are supported?

支持 iOS 17 及以上的 iPhone（包括 iPhone Duo），以及 iPadOS 17 及以上的 iPad。Apple Watch 版本将在未来推出。

iPhone with iOS 17 or later (including iPhone Duo), and iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. An Apple Watch version is coming in a future update.
